📚 Understand Your Dashboard

Click on each section to learn more about data sources, calculations, and how it's used

📡 Data Source

Provider: ESRI World Imagery (powered by WorldView, GeoEye, Landsat satellites)

Update Frequency: 1-6 months depending on location

Resolution: High-resolution RGB imagery (up to 30cm per pixel)

🔍 How We Use This Data

  • Field Verification: Visual confirmation of field boundaries and location
  • Crop Pattern Detection: Identifying visible rows, bare soil patches, or stress areas
  • Context for NDVI: Complementing spectral data with visual imagery
  • Historical Comparison: Track visual changes over seasons (when imagery updates)

📊 Dashboard Integration

The satellite view is interactive - you can zoom in/out and pan to explore your field. It provides geographical context for all other data points displayed in the dashboard.

Note: This is optical imagery, not the same as NDVI (which uses infrared bands).

📡 Data Source

Provider: Sentinel-2 / Landsat (via simulated data in this version)

NDVI Formula: (NIR - Red) / (NIR + Red)

Range: -1.0 to +1.0 (vegetation typically 0.2 to 0.8)

🌡️ What is a Heat Map?

A heat map is a color-coded grid that shows vegetation health across your field:

  • Dark Blue (0.0-0.2): No vegetation / Bare soil
  • Blue (0.2-0.3): Very sparse crops / Early emergence
  • Teal (0.3-0.4): Moderate vegetation / Growing crops
  • Green (0.4-0.6): Healthy crops / Good canopy
  • Bright Green (0.6+): Very healthy / Dense vegetation

🔍 How We Obtain This Data

NDVI is calculated from satellite images using Near-Infrared (NIR) and Red light bands. Healthy plants reflect more NIR and absorb more Red light, resulting in higher NDVI values.

📊 How We Use This Data in the Dashboard

  1. 3D Field Visualization: Each plant in the 3D model gets its color and health from the corresponding NDVI grid cell
  2. Crop Health Assessment: NDVI values feed into the digital twin's health parameter (0-100%)
  3. Stress Detection: Low NDVI areas (yellow/brown in 3D) indicate water or nutrient stress
  4. Spatial Variability: Shows which parts of your field are performing better or worse

Real-time Link: In Live Data Mode, NDVI directly influences plant appearance and density in the 3D field!

🤖 How Predictions are Made

  1. Step 1: Collect 30 days of historical soil moisture from NASA POWER

  3. Step 3: Generate 7-day forecast with confidence intervals
  4. Step 4: Adjust predictions based on upcoming weather (rainfall, temperature)

🎯 What is the Digital Twin?

The 3D Wheat Crop Field is a true digital twin - a virtual replica of your real field that updates in real-time based on satellite data, weather, and growth models.

🌡️ GDD & Auto-Stage Selection

GDD (Growing Degree Days) is a heat accumulation metric that predicts plant growth:

Growth Stage GDD Range Approx Days What's Happening
🌱 Emergence 0 - 180 0-10 days Seed germination, first leaves
🌿 Tillering 180 - 395 10-30 days Multiple stems form
📏 Stem Elongation 395 - 705 30-50 days Rapid height growth
🌾 Booting 705 - 990 50-60 days Wheat head forms inside stem
🌾 Heading 990 - 1425 60-80 days Wheat head emerges, flowering
🌾 Grain Filling 1425+ 80-100+ days Grains develop and mature

📊 How GDD is Calculated

Formula: Daily GDD = Temperature (°C) - Base Temperature (0°C for wheat)

Example: If today is 25°C, you add 25 GDD. Tomorrow is 18°C, you add 18 GDD. Total = 43 GDD.

🧠 AI Model

Type: Q-Learning Reinforcement Learning Agent

Training: Pre-trained on wheat farming scenarios

Goal: Maximize crop health while minimizing resource waste

📊 How Recommendations are Generated

  1. Input Data: Soil moisture forecast (7 days), current weather, crop stage, historical actions
  2. State Analysis: Agent evaluates current field conditions and predicted trajectory
  3. Action Selection: Chooses best action (irrigate, fertilize, or wait) for each day
  4. Optimization: Balances immediate needs vs. long-term crop health

🎯 Types of Recommendations

  • Irrigate: When predicted moisture drops below 30% threshold
  • Fertilize: During key growth stages (Tillering, Stem Elongation)
  • Wait/Monitor: When conditions are stable and no action needed

📈 Data Sources Used

Recommendations integrate multiple data streams:

  • Prophet Soil Moisture Forecast: 7-day predictions guide timing
  • Weather Data: Rainfall probability affects irrigation decisions
  • Crop Stage (GDD): Growth phase determines fertilizer needs
  • Historical Performance: Q-Learning learns from past results

💡 How to Use Recommendations

Each recommendation includes:

  • Day: When to take action (Day 1 = tomorrow)
  • Predicted Soil Moisture: Expected moisture level on that day
  • Action: What to do (irrigate, fertilize, wait)
  • Reasoning: Why the AI suggests this action

Note: These are recommendations, not commands. Always combine with your local knowledge and field observations!
